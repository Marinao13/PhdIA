6.5
General Moment Summability
In Section 5.5 we have introduced general pairs of integral operators with
features similar to those of Laplace and Borel operator. Here we shall show
that each such pair deﬁnes a summability method, and that all methods
corresponding to kernels of a ﬁxed order k > 0 are equivalent in the sense
that they sum the same formal power series to the same analytic functions.
However, observe that we do not claim that two such kernels of the same
order represent the sum on exactly the same sectorial region. Moreover, the
two summability methods may be inequivalent for series other than power
series.
