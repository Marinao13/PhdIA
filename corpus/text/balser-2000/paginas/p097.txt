5.2
Borel Operators
Assume that τ ∈R, k, r > 0, and 0 < ε < π are given. Let γk(τ) denote the
path from the origin along arg z = τ + (ε + π)/(2k) to some z1 of modulus
r, then along the circle |z| = r to the ray arg z = τ −(ε+π)/(2k), and back
to the origin along this ray. In other words, the path γk(τ) is the boundary
of a sector with bisecting direction τ, ﬁnite radius, and opening slightly
larger than π/(2k), and the orientation is negative. The dependence of the
path on ε and r will be inessential and therefore is not displayed.

with |τ −d| < (α −π/k)/2, we may choose ε and r so small that γk(τ) ﬁts
into the region G. If f(z) is holomorphic in G and bounded at the origin,
we deﬁne the Borel transform of f of order k by the integral
