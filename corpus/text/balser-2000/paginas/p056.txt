3. Transformations of the form T(z) = diag [zp1/q, . . . , zpν/q], with pk ∈
Z, q ∈N, will be called shearing transformations. If q = 1, we say
that T(z) is an unramiﬁed shearing transformation.
4. For T(z) = exp[q(z)]I, with q(z) a scalar polynomial in z, we speak
of a scalar exponential shift. Note that such a transformation is holo-
morphic at the origin, but is essentially singular at inﬁnity. However,
since T(z) commutes with A(z), the transformed system has coeﬃ-
cient matrix A(z)−zq′(z)I and hence is again meromorphic at inﬁnity.
