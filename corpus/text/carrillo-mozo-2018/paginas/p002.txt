The plan of the paper is as follows: in Section 2, the notions of asymptotic expansions and summability, 
both in the one variable, and in the monomial case, are reviewed. Monomial Borel and Laplace transforms 
are deﬁned and investigated in Section 3, in order to introduce the summation methods using these operator 
in Section 4. The main result of this work, Theorem 4.1, is stated and proved here. Finally, Section 5 provides 
examples of application to a family of partial diﬀerential equations. A possible further development, in order 
to deﬁne multisummability in the monomial case, is sketched in Section 6.
