The use of Laplace and Borel transforms of arbitrary positive order allows us to generalize
Theorem 4.4 for arbitrary sectors. The idea of the proof for the Roumieu case [12, Th. 4.2]
applies to the Beurling case, we include it for the sake of completeness. We note that this result
may also be deduced from the results in [4] about classes with non-uniform asymptotics, but we
think it is interesting to provide an argument contained in our framework. This procedure makes
Theorem 4.2 necessary.
