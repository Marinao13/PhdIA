Regarding the Beurling case, A. Debrouwere [3, Th. 7.4] first characterized the surjectivity of
the asymptotic Borel map in the right half-plane for regular sequences, and later on he completely
solved the problem for non-uniform asymptotics, and provided global extension operators for
γ < γ(M) in the case with uniform estimates, see [4]. We will present a different technique
in order to treat the problem for classes with uniform estimates, following the same ideas as in
the Roumieu case [12], which rest on the use of ramified Borel and Laplace integral transforms.
In order to do this, we need to prove Theorem 4.2, which slightly improves both a result of J.
Schmets and M. Valdivia [24], Theorem 4.1 in this paper, and the implication (i) ⇒(iii) of the
aforementioned result of A. Debrouwere (Theorem 4.4 in this paper). Finally, the new condition
(sm) is also valid in order to prove surjectivity for Beurling classes as long as 0 < γ < γ(M),
thanks to a technique of J. Chaumat and A. M. Chollet [2] already applied by V. Thilliez [26,
Th. 3.4.1] for strongly regular sequences.
The paper is organized as follows. Section 2 contains the preliminaries, namely: the main
information concerning weight sequences, indices or auxiliary functions associated with them, dif-
ferent ultraholomorphic classes and the asymptotic Borel map, and the known result for Roumieu
classes by the use of optimal flat functions. In Section 3 we obtain the new surjectivity results
for Roumieu classes defined by weight sequences satisfying (sm), while Section 4 contains the
improvements for Beurling classes, according to whether (dc) or (sm) is assumed.
The results included in this paper are part of the Ph.D. dissertation of the second author [16,
Ch. 3], defended at the University of Valladolid (Spain) under the advice of the third author.
