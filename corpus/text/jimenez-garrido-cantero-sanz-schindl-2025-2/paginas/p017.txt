In a recent work, A. Debrouwere [3] characterized the surjectivity of the asymptotic Borel
map in the right half-plane for regular sequences in the terms of the gamma index associated
with this sequence.
Theorem 4.4 ([3], Theorem 7.4). Suppose c
M is a regular sequence. The following are equivalent:
(i) The Borel map eB: A(c
M)(S1) →C[[z]](M) is surjective.
(ii) There exists a global extension operator UM : C[[z]](M) →A(c
M)(S1).
(iii) γ(M) > 1.
Note that the implication (ii) ⇒(iii) corresponds to Theorem 4.1 for r = 1, and that
(iii) ⇒(i) was obtained by V. Thilliez, as already mentioned. Also, the implication (i) ⇒(iii)
is slightly weaker than our previous result applied for r = 1. However, the full equivalence is a
powerful result, as (ii) is deduced from any of the other two conditions.
