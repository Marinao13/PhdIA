STUDIA MATHEMATICA 224 (2) (2014)
Composition in ultradiﬀerentiable classes
by
Armin Rainer and Gerhard Schindl (Wien)
Abstract. We characterize stability under composition of ultradiﬀerentiable classes
deﬁned by weight sequences M, by weight functions ω, and, more generally, by weight
matrices M, and investigate continuity of composition (g, f) 7→f ◦g. In addition, we
represent the Beurling space E(ω) and the Roumieu space E{ω} as intersection and union
of spaces E(M) and E{M} for associated weight sequences, respectively.
1. Introduction. This paper arose from our wish to characterize sta-
bility under composition of Denjoy–Carleman classes E{M} and E(M). For
these classes we have developed a calculus in inﬁnite dimensions beyond Ba-
nach spaces in [24, 26, 25] which is heavily based on composition: A smooth
mapping f is of class E{M} if and only if f ◦p ∈E{M} for all E{M} Banach
plots (i.e., mappings deﬁned in open subsets of Banach spaces); similarly for
E(M). Sometimes curves suﬃce.
