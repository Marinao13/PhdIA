Abstract We characterize stability under composition, inversion, and solution of ordinary
differential equations for ultradifferentiable classes, and prove that all these stability proper-
ties are equivalent.

1 Introduction
Let F denote some class of smooth mappings between non-empty open subsets of Euclidean
spaces (of possibly different dimension). We say that
– F is stable under composition if the composite of any two F-mappings g : U →V and
f : V →W is an F-mapping f ◦g : U →W.
– F is stable under solving ordinary differential equations (ODEs) if for any F-mapping
f : R × Rn →Rn the solution of the initial value problem x′ = f (t, x), x(0) = x0 ∈Rn
is of class F wherever it exists.
– F is stable under inversion if for any F-mapping f : Rm ⊇U →V ⊆Rn so that
f ′(x0) ∈L(Rm, Rn) is invertible at x0 ∈U there exist neighborhoods x0 ∈U0 ⊆U and
f (x0) ∈V0 ⊆V and an F-mapping g : V0 →U0 such that f ◦g = idV0.
– F is inverse closed if 1/f ∈F(U) for each non-vanishing f ∈F(U).
