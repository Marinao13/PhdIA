(a) Theorem 5.4 applies to M ≡Ω= {W x : x > 0}, when Ωis associated with a given weight
function ω ∈W0 (see Section 2.6). In this situation this theorem is consistent with the
known results from [1] and [11]: On the one hand Ωsatisﬁes all requirements from assertion
(i) (for both types) provided that ω ∈W, see (2.3) and Corollary 3.3. On the other hand,
we have in this situation

valid, more precisely even with the value 0.
