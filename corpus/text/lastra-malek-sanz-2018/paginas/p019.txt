6.2
Results on quasi-analyticity in ultraholomorphic classes
We restrict our attention to the one-variable case, although the next results are available also
for functions of several variables (see [12]). First, quasi-analytic Carleman classes are deﬁned.
Deﬁnition 6.6. Let S be a sector in R. We say that AM(S) is quasi-analytic if the conditions:
(i) f ∈AM(S), and
(ii) B(f) is the null sequence (or f admits the null series as asymptotic expansion in S),
together imply that f is the null function in S.
Characterizations of quasi-analyticity are available for general sequences M in [12], but we
will focus on the case of strongly regular sequences, in which the following version of Watson’s
lemma may be obtained.
