Theorem 1.2.36 ([13], Th. 1.9.5, Characterization Theorem for regularly varying sequences).
Let a = (ap)p∈N be a regularly varying sequence of positive numbers. Then there exists ρ ∈R
such that the function g(λ) in (1.14) is equal to λρ.
In this case, ρ is called the index of regular variation of a.
The next theorem makes possible to apply the results about the theory of regularly varying
functions to regularly varying sequences.
Theorem 1.2.37 ([13], Th. 1.9.5). Let a = (ap)p∈N be a sequence of positive numbers. The
sequence a is regularly varying if and only if the function fa(x) = a⌊x⌋for x ≥1 is regularly
varying.
From this embedding result, we deduce that the convergence of the limit in (1.14) is uniform
in the compact sets of (0, ∞) and we see that regularly varying sequences also admit a very
convenient representation.
