Remark 4.2.12. By the previous proposition, if L satises (2.23), which is valid whenever L
admits a nonzero proximate order, we have seen that if ω(L) < ω(M), M and L are comparable
but not equivalent.
Consequently, in our framework, comparability conditions need only to be assumed when
ω(M) = ω(L). In this situation, according to the last result, it is natural to assume that M/L
is a weight sequence or, equivalently, that M/L is (lc) and L ≾M and L ̸≈M, which can not
be deduced from the regularity of M and L (see Example 4.2.4). Note that this is not a weird
condition.
