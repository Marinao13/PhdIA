1.2.2
Proximate orders and smooth variation
In order to study quasianalyticity of the ultraholomorphic classes (see Section 3.2) and to con-
struct kernels of summability in this context (see Section 4.1) we need to introduce the notion
of proximate order, appearing in the theory of growth of entire functions and developed, among
others, by G. Valiron [103], B. Ja. Levin [63] and A. A. Goldberg and I. V. Ostrosvkii [32]. In
this dissertation, a prominent role is played by the results of L. S. Maergoiz [65]. The concept of
proximate order, its elementary properties, its relation to regular variation and the main results
of L. S. Maergoiz are presented in this subsection.
