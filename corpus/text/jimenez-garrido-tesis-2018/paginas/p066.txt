In particular, for strongly regular sequences, β(m) = γ(M), µ(m) = ω(M), ρ(m), α(m) ∈
(0, ∞). In the context of O-regular variation, it is usual to name the class of positive func-
tions having the Matuszewska indices in certain range: if α(f) < ∞, f is said to be of bounded
increase and if β(f) > 0, f is said to be of positive increase. The same terminology can be
adopted for sequences, then a nondecreasing sequence m is strongly regular if and only if it is of
bounded and positive increase.
