It should be highlighted that the aforementioned results by A. Rainer and the fourth
author were established for ultradifferentiable classes defined in terms of a weight matrix.
This approach provides a unified framework that generalizes both the weight sequence
and the weight function settings. It is expected that the characterization results obtained
in this paper can be extended to ultraholomorphic classes defined in terms of weight
matrices, considered in [3, 4]. This generalization is currently part of an ongoing work
in progress.
The structure of this work is organized as follows. We begin with a review of weight
sequences and their properties, followed by the definition of the ultraholomorphic classes
and the construction of the characteristic functions mentioned above. In Section 5, we
present the auxiliary formulas for the expansion of products and compositions. These re-
sults lead to Section 6, where we characterize the closure under point-wise multiplication
via the (alg) condition. Finally, we address the closure under composition, establishing
the equivalence between this stability property and the (FdB) condition.
