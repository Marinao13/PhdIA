Remark 3.5. Let M be a weight sequence with γ(M) > 0. From the previous two
results one easily concludes that M satisfies (dc) (respectively, (sm)) if, and only if, for
every kernel function e associated with an optimal {M}-flat function G in some Sγ one
has (µp(e))p ≈M (resp. (µp(e))p ≈M +1).
