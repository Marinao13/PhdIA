by weight matrices
The aim of this section is to generalize and extend the stability result of Ider and
Siddiqi [25, Thm. 1], valid for Carleman-Roumieu ultraholomorphic classes in sectors not
wider than a half-plane. We give the proof in the general weight matrix setting, we get rid
of the restriction on the opening of the sector (thanks to the construction of characteristic
functions in arbitrary sectors), and we extend the list of stability properties.
Our main result is concerned with several stability properties which will be deﬁned
